# Third-party notices

## Natural Earth

`voyage_lab/data/land.geojson` is a regional subset of Natural Earth 1:50m land, version 5.1.2. Natural Earth data is public domain. Original contributors include Tom Patterson and Nathaniel Vaughn Kelso. Source URL, download hash, and transformation are recorded in `voyage_lab/data/land-provenance.json`.

- [Terms of use](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/)
- [Source dataset](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/tree/v5.1.2)

The coastline is coarse and incomplete for navigation. It is used for a research exclusion check and an offline orientation map. It is not a chart, clearance assessment, harbor approach, or navigability guarantee.
